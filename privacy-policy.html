1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how BoronValley Private Limited ("BoronValley", "we", "us", "our"), a company incorporated in India, collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit this website. It also covers how you can contact us.

Our products, such as NestEaze, may have their own privacy policies. Where they do, those policies apply to the use of those products.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message when you contact us by email or through a form.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URLs and the date and time of your visit, collected through server logs and cookies.
  • Information from partners: limited business contact information shared with us by partners or investors in the normal course of business.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and communicate with you
  • To operate, maintain, secure and improve our website
  • To understand how visitors use the website (aggregated analytics)
  • To meet legal, regulatory and tax obligations
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal basis & consent

We process personal data in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by writing to us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the website work. With your permission, we may also use analytics cookies to understand usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work properly without them.

6. Sharing of information

We share personal data only when necessary:

  • With trusted service providers (hosting, email, analytics) who process data for us under confidentiality obligations
  • With our group products, where you have asked to be connected to them
  • With government or law enforcement authorities where required by law
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to equivalent protections

7. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as long as required by law. After that, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and secure cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Withdraw consent
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India

10. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party links

Our website links to other websites, including nesteaze.com. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. Please read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
